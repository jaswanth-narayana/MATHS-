Practical Budgeting Recommendations for Students

  • Establish a Weekly Allowance: Setting a weekly limit pegged close to the median daily expense helps curb impulsive spending on non-essential cafeteria items or entertainment.
  • Group Travel & Study Material Sharing: Commute and educational expenses can be cut significantly through ride-sharing or digital resource circulation.
  • Active Tracking: Students who actively log daily expenditures remain within the middle 50% spending bracket (IQR), avoiding extreme debt or budget shock.
